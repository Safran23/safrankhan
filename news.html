Successfully Passed My Comprehensive Exam

After four days of written exams, followed by a 2.5 hour oral exam, a 30 minute presentation, and another 30 minutes of questions and answers, I successfully passed my PhD comprehensive exam.

It was a long, demanding, and unforgettable experience, but also a major milestone in my PhD journey. Looking back, it was one of those moments that tested both preparation and perseverance. And yes, it all happened on Valentine’s Day, so that ended up being my Valentine for 2025.
